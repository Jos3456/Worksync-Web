Getting Started

How do I sign up for WorkSync?

To create a WorkSync account:

  1. Go to the Sign Up page.
    • Enter your full name, work email, and create a password.
  2. Click Create Account.
  3. Access dashboard

How do I clock in/out?

On the Dashboard:

  1. Click Clock In when you start work.
  2. Click Clock Out when you finish.
  3. Your total hours are calculated automatically.

Troubleshooting

I can't log in, what should i do?

If the system didn’t register your clock in/out:

  • Ensure you're using the correct email and password.
  • Reset your password via Forgot Password if needed.
  • Clear your browser cache or try a different browser.
  • Contact IT support if the issue persists.

My attendance wasn't recorded. How do I fix this?

If the system didn’t register your clock in/out:

  1. Check your Attendance Log for discrepancies.
  2. Notify your manager to manually correct the record.
  3. Provide details (date, approximate time) for verification.

Account Security

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

To reset your password:

  1. Go to Forgot Password.
  2. Enter your registered email address.
  3. Click the reset link sent to your email.
  4. Create a new password (minimum 8 characters).

Tip: Use a password manager to store credentials securely.

How do I update my profile information?

To edit your profile:

  1. Log in and go to your Dashboard.
  2. Click Edit Profile in the side panel.
  3. Update your phone number, profile picture, etc.
  4. Click Save Changes.

Note: Some fields (like name/email) may require admin approval.
